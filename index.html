What owners ask before starting

Is this only for restaurants?

Not at all. NXAVERSE builds for any small business — salons, contractors, clinics, boutiques, gyms, cafés, freelancers, and dozens more. The AI agent adapts the pages and features to what you do, whether that's booking appointments, sending quotes, taking orders, or showing off a portfolio.

What does the AI agent actually do?

Two things. First, it builds: describe your business and it drafts your pages, copy, and the right features in minutes — you can try it live above. Second, it works inside your finished site: a 24/7 assistant answers customer questions, smart forms capture leads, and the content stays fresh and findable on Google.

If AI builds it, why do I need a team?

AI gets you most of the way fast, but the last mile matters. The NXAVERSE team reviews every draft, polishes the design, connects your domain, email and payments, and stays on call afterward. You get the speed of AI and the judgment of real people who do this every day.

How long does it take and how do I start?

Most sites go live within days. Start by chatting with Nova above, or fill in the short form below — the NXAVERSE team will reach out within one business day to build your site with you. You can also visit nxaverse.com any time.
